# Angel's Magic Stone delta Patch Notes

Complete English translation of `PATCH_NOTE_KR.md`, ordered from the oldest record to the newest.

## 1.0

Fixed a bug that added AI players to the lobby.

Game modes can now be edited after the lobby is made public.

For four or fewer players, Defense Field cooldown 225 -> 180 seconds; Defense Field can now hold two stacks.

For four or more players, New World cooldown 3600 -> 2400 seconds.

Added Round 9 (experimental).

### Global changes

The range of runes applied as nearby auras increases from 4.5 to 6. Affected runes: Defense Reduction, Magic Penetration, Physical Armor, Magic Resistance, Haste, Fortitude, Cleanliness, and Absorption.

Defense Field cooldown is now 180 seconds at all difficulties, with a 10-second reuse delay between stacks to prevent recasting while invulnerable.

Moon Well healing-skill cast range 2 -> 4.

Rune [Growth] purchasers can now draw cards from the Magic Stone.

Added Apocalypse difficulty. Except for Round 9, it differs little from Hell difficulty.
